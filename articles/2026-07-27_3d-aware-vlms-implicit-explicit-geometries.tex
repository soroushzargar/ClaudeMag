  3D-Aware VLMs with Implicit and Explicit Geometries}\par\vspace{4pt}

{\large\itshape\raggedright
  VLM-IE3D Equips Vision-Language Models with Two Complementary
  Geometric Representations Derived Purely from RGB Video,
  Setting New State-of-the-Art on Four 3D Spatial Benchmarks}\par\vspace{6pt}

{\small\textbf{By} Wenhao Li, Xueying Jiang, Quanhao Qian, Deli Zhao,
  Ran Xu, Shijian Lu, Gongjie Zhang
  (Nanyang Technological University)}\par\vspace{2pt}

{\small\color{keywordgray}arXiv:2607.21595 \textbullet{}
  ECCV 2026 \textbullet{} July 23, 2026}
\par\vspace{2pt}\noindent{\color{rulered}\rule{\columnwidth}{0.6pt}}\vspace{6pt}

Vision-language models have demonstrated impressive language and 2D
visual understanding but struggle when tasks demand precise 3D spatial
reasoning. A new ECCV 2026 paper from Nanyang Technological University
introduces \textbf{VLM-IE3D}, which equips standard VLMs with two
complementary 3D geometric representations---implicit and explicit---
derived from RGB video alone, with no depth sensors or 3D supervision.
The framework achieves state-of-the-art performance on 3D video detection,
3D visual grounding, 3D dense captioning, and spatial reasoning benchmarks.

\section{The Big Picture}

Modern VLMs are trained on billions of image-text pairs, giving them
powerful 2D visual understanding and broad world knowledge. Yet 3D
spatial tasks---locating objects relative to each other in 3D space,
predicting 3D bounding boxes, or answering questions about scene
geometry---remain a persistent weakness. These tasks require a model
to reason about depth, surface orientation, and object extent that
is only partially recoverable from 2D images.

Existing solutions typically assume access to depth sensors or
LiDAR at inference time, which is unrealistic for camera-only
deployments. VLM-IE3D bridges this gap: starting from ordinary RGB
video, it derives two complementary geometric representations and
injects them into the VLM as geometric token streams.

\section{Why Existing Approaches Fall Short}

Three prior strategies have been tried with limited success:

\textbf{Monocular depth augmentation:} Estimated depth maps are fed
alongside RGB images. Depth estimation errors propagate to downstream
tasks, and estimated depth lacks surface-normal precision required
for grounding.

\textbf{Implicit-only geometry:} Some recent VLMs inject geometric
priors learned from video pretraining via contrastive objectives.
These provide coarse global layout but lack the local precision needed
for fine-grained 3D grounding---a deficiency VLM-IE3D directly addresses
with explicit geometry tokens.

\textbf{Explicit-only geometry:} Sparse point clouds from structure-from-motion
(SfM) encode precise local structure but lack the holistic scene
layout captured by implicit priors. Without the global context,
reasoning about scene-level spatial relationships degrades.

VLM-IE3D combines both: implicit for global layout,
explicit for local precision.

\section{Core Mechanism}

\textbf{Implicit Geometry Tokens (IGTs)} are produced by a lightweight
video encoder that processes $T$ frames jointly. The encoder attends
to temporal motion parallax and relative depth ordering cues to produce
a compact set of $N_\mathrm{IGT}$ global geometric tokens. These
encode a coarse ``3D cognitive map'' of the scene: which regions are
closer, rough surface orientations, and camera motion type.

\textbf{Explicit Geometry Tokens (EGTs)} are produced by running a
differentiable SfM module on the input frames to recover a sparse
point cloud $\mathcal{P} = \{(\mathbf{p}_i, \mathbf{n}_i)\}$.
The point cloud is voxelized into a 3D feature grid and encoded by
a sparse 3D convolutional encoder into $N_\mathrm{EGT}$ tokens.
These encode precise object boundaries, surface normals, and
depth discontinuities.

A \textbf{3D-aware adapter} fuses IGTs and EGTs with the VLM's
2D visual tokens using cross-attention with geometry-conditioned masks
that spatially align explicit geometric tokens with their 2D image
counterparts.

\section{Technical Formulation}

Given video $V = \{f_1, \ldots, f_T\}$, let $Z_\mathrm{2D} \in \mathbb{R}^{N_\mathrm{2D} \times d}$
be the 2D visual tokens from the VLM's encoder. The IGT and EGT
encoders produce:

\[
Z_\mathrm{IGT} = \phi_\mathrm{IG}(V) \in \mathbb{R}^{N_\mathrm{IGT} \times d}
\]
\[
Z_\mathrm{EGT} = \phi_\mathrm{EG}(\mathcal{P}(V)) \in \mathbb{R}^{N_\mathrm{EGT} \times d}
\]

The 3D-aware adapter computes fused tokens:

\[
Z_\mathrm{fused} = \mathrm{Attn}\!\left(Q = Z_\mathrm{2D},\;
  K = V = [Z_\mathrm{IGT};\, Z_\mathrm{EGT};\, Z_\mathrm{2D}]\right) + Z_\mathrm{2D}
\]

with a geometry-conditioned attention mask $\mathbf{M} \in \{0,-\infty\}^{N_\mathrm{2D} \times (N_\mathrm{IGT}+N_\mathrm{EGT}+N_\mathrm{2D})}$
where $M_{ij} = 0$ if EGT token $j$ projects within spatial radius $r$
of 2D token $i$, and $-\infty$ otherwise (no restriction on IGT
tokens, which are global). The fused representation $Z_\mathrm{fused}$
concatenated with the text query tokens is passed to the frozen VLM backbone.

\section{Learning or Inference Procedure}

Training proceeds in two stages:

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2pt,parsep=0pt]
  \item \textbf{Geometric pretraining:} $\phi_\mathrm{IG}$ and $\phi_\mathrm{EG}$
    are pretrained on 3D video datasets (ScanNet, ARKitScenes) with
    reconstruction objectives: IGTs supervised to predict surface
    orientation distributions, EGTs supervised via point cloud
    completion loss.
  \item \textbf{End-to-end instruction tuning:} The full model
    (adapter $+$ pretrained geometry encoders $+$ frozen VLM) is
    fine-tuned on 3D instruction-following data with a standard
    language modeling loss.
\end{enumerate}

At inference, only the RGB video is required. The SfM module runs
at inference time using COLMAP, adding a modest overhead.

\section{What the Guarantee Says}

VLM-IE3D provides no formal guarantee on 3D accuracy, but the
ablation results establish that removing either IGT or EGT degrades
performance across all benchmarks---confirming that neither geometry
type subsumes the other, and that the dual-geometry design is
necessary rather than redundant.

\section{Ablations and Interpretation}

\textbf{IGT only:} Removes EGT. ScanRefer (grounding) degrades
by 6.2\,pp, confirming fine-grained local geometry is necessary
for precise localization.

\textbf{EGT only:} Removes IGT. ScanQA (spatial reasoning) degrades
by 4.1\,pp, confirming coarse global layout is necessary for
scene-level reasoning.

\textbf{Na\"ive concatenation:} Concatenates IGT and EGT without
the geometry-conditioned mask. Average performance degrades by 3.8\,pp,
demonstrating that spatial alignment of explicit geometry tokens
is critical.

\textbf{SfM quality:} Replacing COLMAP with a faster but noisier SfM
variant reduces EmbodiedScan mAP by 2.3\,pp, confirming a moderate
sensitivity to point cloud quality.
